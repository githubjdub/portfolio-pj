    Composition notes

    Open positions at the selected price mark, weighted by market value; sold positions and dividend cash are excluded. Today's quote-based snapshot is provisional. Current sector classifications are used for every snapshot. Funds / ETFs are grouped separately, without looking through their holdings.

    For each security, total P&L is position value plus sale proceeds and dividends minus all buy costs through the snapshot. Return on buys is P&L divided by those buy costs. Average buy and sell prices are not the cost basis of the remaining shares. Performance charts use time-weighted returns over their own selected period.

    Chart notes

    Range and weighting controls update every performance chart and risk metric. Manager-Weighted uses your actual trades and position sizes, retaining dividends as cash. Equal-Weighted is a hypothetical daily-rebalanced, equal-dollar basket of the securities held each session; rebalancing costs and taxes are not modeled. Quarterly returns compound the selected series. Today's endpoint is provisional when it uses regular-session quotes. Risk metrics use completed daily returns. Arrows mark your logged transactions. The total-return benchmark reinvests dividends.
